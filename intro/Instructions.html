Imagine that you are doing a personal Bible study. As you read, there may be parts of the passage that you do not understand or would like to explore further. During this study, an AI chat system will be available to help you with your questions.

You will complete two Bible-study tasks. In each task, you will read a Bible passage, ask the chat system at least one question, and then evaluate the system you have just used.

How to use the chat system

  1. Read the displayed Bible passage carefully.
  2. Type a question into the box labelled Your question.
  3. Press Enter or select the ↑ send button. Use Shift + Enter to start a new line.
  4. Read the response. You may ask additional questions or request clarification. If you are unsure what to ask, you may open the optional suggested question on the task page.
  5. You must ask at least one question and receive a response before Continue appears. When you have finished, select it to proceed.
