You are invited to take part in a research study titled {{ study_title }}, conducted by {{ researcher_name }} from {{ researcher_affiliation }}.

Purpose of the study

This study compares people's experiences of two AI chat systems used for personal Bible study. It examines perceived usefulness, clarity, trust, contextual support, and overall experience.

What to expect and time commitment

You will complete a background questionnaire, two Bible-study tasks, an evaluation after each task, and a final comparison. The whole study takes approximately 30–50 minutes.

Anonymous data collection

We will not ask for your name, contact details, or other direct identifiers. The system creates a random technical response code only to connect the different parts of your response. The code is not connected to your identity, and no code-to-identity list will be created.

Please do not enter names, contact details, addresses, usernames, or other information that could identify you or another person. Obvious contact details in free-text responses will be blocked before they are stored or sent to the AI provider.

Potential risks

The AI systems are experimental and may give inaccurate, incomplete, or overly confident answers, including on theologically sensitive topics. They are not substitutes for pastoral, theological, medical, legal, or other professional advice.

Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without penalty by closing the survey window. Incomplete responses will not be included in the analysis and will be deleted before analysis. After you submit the completed study, it will no longer be possible to identify and delete your individual response. Required questions must be completed if you choose to continue through the study.
